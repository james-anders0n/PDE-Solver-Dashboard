# Phase 4 — Heston 2D engine

Status: **complete** (19 August 2026)

Phase 4 adds a standalone two-dimensional Heston finite-difference engine,
connects it to the dashboard, and validates it against an independent
semi-analytic Fourier benchmark. The implementation is in
`app/lib/pde-engine/heston.ts`.

## Implemented numerical method

- The state grid is the tensor product of `S ∈ [0,Smax]` and
  `v ∈ [0,vmax]`. Uniform and smooth, focus-fitted nonuniform grids are
  supported. The nonuniform grid places the strike and the initial variance on
  nodes and concentrates resolution around them.
- The mixed derivative `V_Sv` uses the tensor product of the nonuniform
  three-point first-derivative formulas. This is a nine-point stencil on the
  local `S-v` patch and reduces to the standard centred cross stencil on a
  uniform mesh.
- The operator is split into the explicit mixed term and implicit spot and
  variance directions. Both Modified Craig–Sneyd (MCS, `θ=1/3`) and
  Hundsdorfer–Verwer (HV, `θ=1/2+√3/6`) ADI corrections are available.
- At `v=0`, the variance diffusion and mixed derivative vanish. The engine
  applies the resulting reduced PDE and uses a second-order one-sided
  nonuniform approximation for `V_v`. The one extra first-row coupling is
  eliminated algebraically before the tridiagonal variance solve.
- At `vmax`, `V_v=0` is imposed directly. Calls and puts use discounted
  asymptotic Dirichlet values at the spot boundaries.
- The default domains are parameter-aware. `Smax` responds to spot, strike,
  maturity and variance; `vmax` is based on the CIR conditional mean and
  variance with a conservative tail allowance. Both allow explicit overrides.

## Independent benchmark

The benchmark integrates the risk-neutral Heston characteristic function with
96-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature over `[0,120]`. The pricing code is separate
from the finite-difference operator and ADI time stepper. Put prices are
obtained from the independently integrated call through put–call parity.

The standard acceptance fixture is:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---:|
| `S0`, `K`, `T` | `100`, `100`, `1` |
| `r`, `q` | `0.05`, `0` |
| `v0`, `κ`, `θ`, `ξ`, `ρ` | `0.04`, `2`, `0.04`, `0.30`, `-0.70` |
| Fourier call benchmark | `10.3942185652` |

## Published acceptance set

All values below are generated by the automated test fixtures. Point-price
acceptance is `|ΔV(S0,v0,0)| ≤ 5×10⁻³`.

| Fixture and method | Grid `MS × Mv × N` | PDE | Benchmark | Absolute error |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Standard, MCS | `80 × 40 × 160` | `10.3898721` | `10.3942186` | `4.347×10⁻³` |
| Standard, HV | `80 × 40 × 160` | `10.3898654` | `10.3942186` | `4.353×10⁻³` |
| Feller-violating put, MCS | `64 × 32 × 128` | `6.0582325` | `6.0627988` | `4.566×10⁻³` |

The Feller-violating fixture uses `r=0.03`, `q=0.01`, `κ=1.2`, `θ=0.04`,
`ξ=0.50`, `ρ=-0.65`, so `2κθ/ξ²=0.384`. The run is retained, its boundary is
handled by the reduced PDE, and the diagnostic explicitly reports the failed
Feller condition.

## Stability, consistency and convergence evidence

- Uniform tensor refinement `16×8×32 → 32×16×64 → 64×32×128` reduces the
  standard-fixture point error from `1.572` to `0.4913` to `0.1200`. The last
  two observed orders are `1.68` and `2.03`, exceeding the Phase 4 order gate.
- Expanding both state domains by 25%, while preserving approximate mesh
  density, changes the compact validation price by `4.70×10⁻³`.
- At `ρ=0`, MCS and HV differ by only `2.25×10⁻⁵` on the compact validation
  grid.
- The largest recorded tridiagonal residual on the standard MCS fixture is
  below `2×10⁻¹³`.
- The imposed far-variance derivative is zero to floating-point accuracy. The
  standard-grid undershoot is less than `8×10⁻⁴`; the acceptance test permits
  this vanishing discretisation-scale oscillation while refinement drives it
  toward zero.
- Tests cover tensor dimensions, finite values, the cross-stencil declaration,
  both ADI schemes, the degenerate boundary, Feller violation, put–call parity,
  domain expansion, refinement order and the `ρ→0` scheme-consistency limit.

## Dashboard views

Selecting Heston now activates its own `MS × Mv × N` controls and MCS/HV
scheme selector. A solved run displays:

1. `V(S,v,t=0)` as a selectable surface or heatmap;
2. captured `V(S,t)` slices at fixed `v=v0`;
3. captured `V(v,t)` slices at fixed `S=S0`;
4. PDE and Fourier point prices, errors, residuals, Feller ratio and boundary
   diagnostics; and
5. tensor-grid refinement and reproducible exported configuration data.

## Exit gate

The Phase 4 exit criterion is met: both production ADI schemes are stable on
the published standard fixture, the attainable-boundary fixture passes the
same point tolerance, refinement is convergent with observed order above 1.5,
and domain and zero-correlation sensitivity checks remain controlled.
